# Old Age Pension Scheme — Complete Guide

**Scheme Name:** Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS) / State Old Age Pension  
**Nodal Ministry:** Ministry of Rural Development (NSAP); State Social Welfare Departments  
**Governed By:** National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP), 1995; State-level rules  
**Source:** Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India

---

## 1. Overview

The Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS) provides monthly financial assistance to elderly persons belonging to Below Poverty Line (BPL) households. It is a centrally sponsored scheme under the National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP). Most states top up the central share with their own contribution.

The scheme aims to provide social protection to senior citizens who have no or negligible income from other sources, ensuring a dignified livelihood in old age.

---

## 2. Eligibility Criteria

### Central Component (IGNOAPS)
| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 60 years and above |
| **BPL Status** | Must belong to a household included in the BPL list |
| **Residence** | Citizen of India; residing in the applying state/district |
| **Other pension** | Must NOT be receiving any other pension/regular income from government sources (in most states) |

### Enhanced Pension — 80 Years and Above
- Beneficiaries aged **80 years and above** receive an enhanced central share of **₹500/month** (from ₹200/month for 60–79 years).
- Many states offer an additional top-up (e.g., Andhra Pradesh pays ₹2,400/month total).

### State-Specific Variations
Several states have relaxed the BPL requirement or extended coverage to APL senior citizens above a certain age threshold. Check your state's Social Welfare Department portal for local rules.

---

## 3. Pension Amount

| Age Group | Central Government Share | Typical Total (Central + State) |
|---|---|---|
| 60–79 years | ₹200/month | ₹500–₹2,500/month (state-dependent) |
| 80 years and above | ₹500/month | ₹1,000–₹3,000/month (state-dependent) |

---

## 4. Documents Required

1. **Age Proof** (any one):
   - Birth certificate
   - School leaving certificate / mark sheet with date of birth
   - Aadhaar card (if DOB is updated)
   - Passport
   - Certificate from a registered medical practitioner (if no other document available)
2. **Identity Proof:** Aadhaar card (mandatory for DBT), Voter ID
3. **BPL Certificate / BPL List Inclusion Proof:** Copy of ration card showing BPL/AAY category, or certificate from Gram Panchayat / Ward Officer
4. **Residence Proof:** Aadhaar, voter ID, ration card with current address
5. **Bank Account Details:** Passbook copy; account must be Aadhaar-linked for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
6. **Passport-size photograph** (2 copies)
7. **Self-declaration** of no other pension or regular government income
8. **Disability Certificate** (if applicable, for certain state schemes)

## 5. Application Process

### Step 1 — Obtain the Application Form
- **Rural areas:** From Gram Panchayat or Block Development Office (BDO)
- **Urban areas:** From Ward Office / Urban Local Body (ULB)
- **Online:** State Social Welfare Department portal (e.g., nsap.nic.in for IGNOAPS beneficiary search; state portals for new applications)

### Step 2 — Fill and Submit
- Complete the application form with all personal details.
- Attach self-attested copies of all documents.
- Submit at the Gram Panchayat (rural) or Ward Office (urban).
- Obtain an acknowledgement receipt with a date and reference number.

### Step 3 — Verification
- The Gram Panchayat / Ward Officer verifies the application and conducts an in-person visit if needed.
- The application is forwarded to the Block Development Officer / SDM for approval.

### Step 4 — Approval and Disbursement
- Pension is sanctioned within **30–60 days** in most states.
- Amount is directly credited to the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account on a monthly or quarterly basis (frequency varies by state).
- Beneficiary is added to the NSAP MIS (Management Information System) for tracking.

## 6. Common Rejection Reasons and Remedies

Understanding why applications are rejected helps you ensure a successful reapplication:

### 1. Age Proof Not Accepted
- **Rejection reason:** "Submitted document does not conclusively establish age / age proof is not from a recognized authority."
- **Remedy:** Use a school leaving certificate or hospital birth record. If unavailable, a medical certificate from a Civil Surgeon or Registered Medical Practitioner, duly attested, is the last resort. Voter ID alone is often insufficient as age may not match records.

### 2. Not on BPL List
- **Rejection reason:** "Applicant's household is not included in the district BPL list / Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) data."
- **Remedy:** Verify your inclusion in the SECC 2011 database (secc.gov.in). If erroneously excluded, apply for a BPL certificate correction through the Gram Sabha or District Collector's office. Some states allow certificates from the Gram Panchayat head as an interim measure.

### 3. Aadhaar-Bank Account Not Linked
- **Rejection reason:** "DBT transfer failed due to Aadhaar-bank seeding not completed."
- **Remedy:** Visit your bank branch with Aadhaar card and request Aadhaar-account linking. Verify the mapping on the UIDAI portal (uidai.gov.in). This is mandatory for pension credit.

### 4. Receiving Another Government Pension
- **Rejection reason:** "Records indicate applicant is already a beneficiary of [scheme name] pension."
- **Remedy:** If the other pension has ceased or is a different scheme entirely (e.g., EPFO pension vs. social welfare pension), submit documentary proof of cessation. Rules vary by state — some states permit IGNOAPS even if EPFO pension is very small.

### 5. Duplicate Application in System
- **Rejection reason:** "A pension account already exists for this Aadhaar / this beneficiary is already registered in the NSAP database."
- **Remedy:** Contact the Block Development Office with proof of non-receipt of pension. A data correction request can be raised with the district NSAP nodal officer.

### 6. Incomplete or Incorrect Form
- **Rejection reason:** "Application missing nominee details / bank account number / Aadhaar number."
- **Remedy:** Fill all mandatory fields. Bank account number, IFSC code, and 12-digit Aadhaar are typically mandatory. Do not leave any field blank.

### 7. Address Not in Jurisdiction
- **Rejection reason:** "Applicant's residence is outside the jurisdiction of the applying Gram Panchayat / Ward."
- **Remedy:** Apply through the correct Gram Panchayat or Ward Office corresponding to your Aadhaar/voter ID address. If you have recently moved, update Aadhaar address first.

## 7. Appeals and Grievance Redressal

- **Block-level:** Submit written complaint to Block Development Officer (BDO)
- **District-level:** District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO)
- **State-level:** State Social Welfare Commissioner
- **Central-level:** NSAP helpline and grievance portal at nsap.nic.in
- **PM Helpline:** 1800-180-1995 (toll-free, NSAP helpline)
- Response timeline: 30 working days under Citizens' Charter

---

## 8. Scheme Integration and Additional Benefits

Old Age Pension beneficiaries are often automatically linked to:
- **Ayushman Bharat – PM-JAY:** Health insurance coverage up to ₹5 lakh/year
- **PM Ujjwala Yojana:** Free LPG connections for BPL households
- **Annapurna Scheme:** 10 kg free food grains/month for those not covered by NFSA
- **State-level welfare schemes:** Free spectacles, hearing aids, and elder care services under Integrated Programme for Older Persons (IPOP)

---

## 9. Renewal / Continuation

- Pension once sanctioned continues unless:
  - Beneficiary's income increases beyond the prescribed limit
  - Beneficiary becomes recipient of another pension
  - Beneficiary passes away (family must report to the Gram Panchayat/Ward office to de-register and stop further payments)
- **Annual life certificate** (Jeevan Pramaan Patra) must be submitted every November to continue receiving pension without interruption.
