# Ration Card — Guidelines, Application Process, and Rejection Explanations

**Issuing Authority:** District Supply Office / Food, Civil Supplies & Consumer Affairs Department  
**Governed By:** National Food Security Act, 2013; State PDS Control Orders  
**Source:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution, Government of India

---

## 1. Overview

A Ration Card is an official document issued by state governments that entitles households to purchase subsidised food grains and essential commodities through the Public Distribution System (PDS). It also serves as a proof of identity and residence for many government schemes. Under the National Food Security Act (NFSA) 2013, eligible households receive rice, wheat, and coarse cereals at highly subsidised rates.

---

## 2. Types of Ration Cards

| Card Type | Colour | Eligibility | Entitlement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) | Yellow | Poorest of the poor, destitute, landless labourers | 35 kg/family/month at ₹1–₹3/kg |
| Priority Household (PHH) | Orange / Pink | Households below poverty line under NFSA | 5 kg/person/month at ₹1–₹3/kg |
| Below Poverty Line (BPL) | Blue (non-NFSA states) | BPL households outside NFSA coverage | State-determined quantities |
| Above Poverty Line (APL) | White | Households above poverty line | Market price or subsidised (state-specific) |

> **Note:** APL cards are being phased out in many states under NFSA implementation. Check your state's current scheme.

---

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

### General Eligibility
- Must be a permanent resident of the state/UT applying in.
- Must not already possess a ration card in any state.
- Household income must fall within prescribed limits (state-specific):
  - **AAY:** No income criterion; targeted at most vulnerable families.
  - **PHH/BPL:** Annual family income typically below ₹1,00,000–₹1,20,000 (varies by state).
  - **APL:** Above BPL limits; card may or may not confer subsidised grain.
- Must not own more than prescribed agricultural land or assets.

### Priority Categories for AAY/PHH
- Landless agricultural labourers, marginal farmers
- Rural artisans / craftspeople with low income
- Slum dwellers
- Daily wage earners (construction, domestic, etc.)
- Primitive tribal groups
- Persons with disabilities without adequate means of subsistence
- Widows / destitute women / single women
- HIV/AIDS patients
- Disaster-affected families

---

## 4. Documents Required

### For New Application
1. **Proof of Identity** (any one): Aadhaar card, Voter ID, PAN card, Passport
2. **Proof of Residence** (any one): Aadhaar, electricity bill, water bill, rental/lease agreement, bank passbook with address
3. **Income Certificate:** Issued by Tehsildar / Revenue Officer (for BPL/PHH cards)
4. **Caste Certificate** (for SC/ST/OBC category claims): Issued by competent authority
5. **Passport-size photographs** of head of household (2–4 copies)
6. **Self-declaration of non-possession** of ration card in any state
7. **Bank account details** (Aadhaar-linked, for DBT benefits)
8. **Marriage certificate** (for newly married couples forming new household)
9. **Surrender certificate** from previous state (for migrant households)

## 5. Application Process

### Method 1 — Online (Recommended)
1. Visit your state's Food & Civil Supplies Department portal (e.g., mahafood.gov.in for Maharashtra, rcms.ap.gov.in for Andhra Pradesh, tnpds.gov.in for Tamil Nadu).
2. Register with your mobile number (OTP-based verification).
3. Select **"Apply for New Ration Card."**
4. Fill the family details form (head of household + all family members with Aadhaar numbers).
5. Upload scanned copies of required documents.
6. Submit and note the **Application Reference Number.**
7. Track status online; physical verification may be scheduled.
8. Upon approval, download the digital ration card or collect the physical card from the Fair Price Shop (FPS).

### Method 2 — Offline
1. Collect the application form from the nearest District Supply Office or Fair Price Shop.
2. Fill the form carefully in capital letters.
3. Attach self-attested photocopies of all documents.
4. Submit at the District Supply Office with a receiving stamp.
5. Officer conducts field verification within 30 days.
6. On approval, card is dispatched to the registered address or available for collection.

## 7. Common Rejection Reasons and How to Avoid Them

Applications are frequently rejected for the following reasons. This section explains each rejection clearly so you can correct and re-apply:

### 1. Income Exceeds Prescribed Limit
- **Reason stated in rejection:** "Annual household income of ₹X exceeds the BPL/PHH ceiling of ₹1,00,000 applicable in this district."
- **What to do:** If you believe the income certificate is incorrect, apply for a fresh income certificate from the Tehsildar. If income genuinely exceeds limits, apply for an APL card instead.

### 2. Duplicate Application / Existing Card Detected
- **Reason:** "Ration card already exists in the name of a family member / same Aadhaar number linked to another card."
- **What to do:** If you migrated from another state, obtain a **surrender certificate** from that state's supply office and reapply. If it is a data error, visit the District Supply Office with original documents.

### 3. Aadhaar Not Linked or Seeding Failure
- **Reason:** "Aadhaar number could not be seeded / Aadhaar details mismatch with UIDAI database."
- **What to do:** Ensure your name and DOB in the ration card application exactly match your Aadhaar. Visit the nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra to correct discrepancies before reapplying.

### 4. Address Proof Not Accepted
- **Reason:** "Submitted address proof is more than 6 months old / does not show current address."
- **What to do:** Submit a recent utility bill, bank statement, or a fresh rental agreement. Self-declarations without supporting documents are not accepted.

### 5. Missing Income Certificate
- **Reason:** "Income certificate not submitted / certificate not issued by a competent authority."
- **What to do:** Obtain a fresh income certificate from the Tehsildar's office (not a self-declaration). The certificate must be on official letterhead with a seal.

### 6. Non-Resident Status
- **Reason:** "Applicant does not appear to be a permanent resident of this district / state."
- **What to do:** Permanent residents need at least 3–6 months of verifiable stay (utility bills, rental agreements). Migrant workers may be eligible for a portable ration card under One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC) scheme.

### 7. Asset Ownership Exceeds Limit
- **Reason:** "Household owns land/vehicle/property beyond prescribed limit."
- **What to do:** Review state-specific asset exclusion criteria. If assets are joint family assets, a legal partition document may help clarify your individual share.

### 8. Incomplete Application Form
- **Reason:** "Application form is incomplete — mandatory fields (date of birth, Aadhaar, relationship) not filled."
- **What to do:** Fill every field. Cross-check Aadhaar numbers of all family members. Use CAPITAL letters as instructed.

### 9. Field Verification Failure
- **Reason:** "Inspector visited premises; applicant / household not found at the stated address."
- **What to do:** Ensure someone is available at the address during business hours for the verification visit. Provide a mobile number for the inspector to call in advance.

### 10. Mismatched Details Across Documents
- **Reason:** "Name/DOB on submitted documents does not match across Aadhaar, income certificate, and form."
- **What to do:** Get a self-declaration affidavit on ₹100 stamp paper explaining the discrepancy (e.g., spelling variation in name), attested by a notary or gazetted officer.

---

## 8. Appeals and Grievance Redressal

- **First appeal:** District Grievance Redressal Officer (DGRO) — within 30 days of rejection
- **Second appeal:** State Food Commission — within 60 days of DGRO order
- **Online grievance:** pgportal.gov.in, National Food Helpline: **1967** (toll-free)
- **Ombudsman:** Designated under NFSA in each state

---

## 9. One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)

Under ONORC, beneficiaries can purchase subsidised grain from **any Fair Price Shop** in India using Aadhaar-based biometric authentication. This benefits migrant workers and persons who have moved to a different state. No separate application is needed; your existing card becomes portable.

---

## 10. Scheme Integration

Ration card holders (especially BPL/PHH/AAY) are automatically considered for:
- PM Ujjwala Yojana (free LPG connection)
- PM Awas Yojana (housing scheme)
- Mahatma Gandhi NREGS (employment guarantee)
- State health insurance schemes (Ayushman Bharat, state variants)
