# Income Tax — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Department:** Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT)  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Finance, Government of India

---

## 1. Overview of Income Tax in India

Income tax is a direct tax levied by the Central Government on the income earned by individuals, Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs), firms, companies, and other entities during a financial year. The tax is governed by the Income Tax Act, 1961, and administered by the Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) under the Ministry of Finance.

**Financial Year (FY):** April 1 – March 31  
**Assessment Year (AY):** The year following the FY (e.g., FY 2024-25 → AY 2025-26)

---

## 2. Who Must File an Income Tax Return (ITR)?

You are required to file an ITR if:

| Scenario | Condition |
|---|---|
| Individual / HUF | Total gross income exceeds ₹2,50,000 in FY |
| Senior citizen (60–79 years) | Gross income exceeds ₹3,00,000 |
| Super senior citizen (80+ years) | Gross income exceeds ₹5,00,000 |
| New Tax Regime | All individuals eligible if income > ₹3,00,000 (basic exemption limit under new regime) |
| Any person | Has deposited more than ₹1 crore in bank accounts in FY |
| Any person | Has incurred foreign travel expense exceeding ₹2 lakh in FY |
| Any person | Paid electricity bill exceeding ₹1 lakh in FY |
| Any person | Has tax refund due |

---

## 3. Tax Slabs — Old vs New Regime (AY 2025-26)

### Old Tax Regime (with deductions)
| Income Slab | Tax Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹2,50,000 | Nil |
| ₹2,50,001 – ₹5,00,000 | 5% |
| ₹5,00,001 – ₹10,00,000 | 20% |
| Above ₹10,00,000 | 30% |

### New Tax Regime (default from FY 2023-24)
| Income Slab | Tax Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹3,00,000 | Nil |
| ₹3,00,001 – ₹7,00,000 | 5% |
| ₹7,00,001 – ₹10,00,000 | 10% |
| ₹10,00,001 – ₹12,00,000 | 15% |
| ₹12,00,001 – ₹15,00,000 | 20% |
| Above ₹15,00,000 | 30% |

**Standard Deduction:** ₹50,000 (salaried individuals) in both regimes from FY 2024-25.

## 4. Key Deductions and Exemptions (Old Regime Only)

| Section | Deduction | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| 80C | LIC, PPF, ELSS, home loan principal, school fees | ₹1,50,000 |
| 80D | Health insurance premium | ₹25,000 (₹50,000 for senior citizens) |
| 80E | Interest on education loan | Full interest (8 years) |
| 80G | Donations to approved charities | 50%–100% depending on institution |
| 80TTA | Interest on savings bank account | ₹10,000 |
| 80TTB | Interest for senior citizens (savings + FD) | ₹50,000 |
| 24(b) | Home loan interest (self-occupied) | ₹2,00,000 |
| HRA | House Rent Allowance | Actual or formula-based |

## 5. Senior Citizen Tax Benefits and Rebates

### Who is a Senior Citizen?
- **Senior Citizen:** 60 years and above but below 80 years
- **Super Senior Citizen:** 80 years and above

### Specific Benefits
1. **Higher basic exemption limit:** ₹3,00,000 (senior) and ₹5,00,000 (super senior) under old regime.
2. **Section 80D enhanced limit:** Premium up to ₹50,000 (vs ₹25,000 for general public).
3. **Section 80TTB:** Interest income from savings accounts, FDs, and recurring deposits up to ₹50,000 is deductible (vs ₹10,000 under 80TTA for others).
4. **Advance tax exemption:** Senior citizens without business income are exempt from paying advance tax.
5. **No ITR filing if income only from pension/interest:** Super senior citizens (80+) receiving income only from pension and interest are not required to file ITR if tax is deducted at source by employer/bank (under Section 194P, subject to conditions).
6. **Form 15H:** Senior citizens can submit Form 15H to their bank to declare that income is below taxable limit, preventing TDS deduction on FD interest.

---

## 6. Section 87A Rebate

Citizens with **total taxable income up to ₹5,00,000 (old regime)** or **₹7,00,000 (new regime)** are entitled to a tax rebate under Section 87A:
- **Old Regime:** Rebate of up to ₹12,500 — effectively zero tax for income up to ₹5 lakh.
- **New Regime:** Rebate of up to ₹25,000 — zero tax for income up to ₹7 lakh.

This means no income tax is payable even if you fall in the 5% tax slab, provided your total taxable income does not exceed the threshold.

## 7. How to File an ITR — Step by Step

### Online (e-Filing Portal — incometax.gov.in)
1. **Register / Login:** Use PAN as user ID. Register at incometax.gov.in if first time.
2. **Select ITR Form:**
   - **ITR-1 (Sahaj):** Salaried individuals, one house property, other sources — income up to ₹50 lakh
   - **ITR-2:** Capital gains, foreign assets, more than one property
   - **ITR-3:** Business/profession income
   - **ITR-4 (Sugam):** Presumptive business income
3. **Pre-fill and Verify:** The portal auto-populates data from Form 26AS, AIS (Annual Information Statement), and TIS.
4. **Enter Income Details:** Salary, rent, interest, capital gains, etc.
5. **Claim Deductions:** Enter 80C, 80D, and other deductions.
6. **Compute Tax:** Portal calculates tax payable or refund due.
7. **Pay Tax if Due:** Via net banking, UPI, debit card through the portal's payment gateway.
8. **Submit and Verify:** Submit and e-verify using Aadhaar OTP, net banking, or DSC. Physical verification (ITR-V) is an alternative — must reach CPC Bengaluru within 30 days.

### Due Dates
| Category | Due Date |
|---|---|
| Individuals (non-audit) | 31 July of AY |
| Individuals with audit requirement | 31 October of AY |
| Belated return | 31 December of AY (with late fee) |

## 8. TDS (Tax Deducted at Source)

- Employers deduct TDS from salary monthly based on projected annual income.
- Banks deduct TDS on FD interest exceeding ₹40,000/year (₹50,000 for senior citizens) — 10% TDS if PAN is provided; 20% if not.
- TDS credits can be verified in **Form 26AS** and **AIS** on the e-filing portal.
- Excess TDS deducted is refunded upon filing ITR.

## 9. Common Rejection / Processing Issues and Solutions

### 1. ITR Defective Notice (Section 139(9))
- **Reason:** "Return is defective due to missing schedules / incorrect ITR form / mismatch in income heads."
- **What to do:** Respond to the notice within **15 days** on the e-filing portal by filing a revised return in the correct ITR form. Failure to respond makes the return void.

### 2. Demand Notice — Tax Mismatch
- **Reason:** "Income reported in ITR does not match data in AIS/Form 26AS."
- **What to do:** Cross-check AIS on the e-filing portal. Respond online to each AIS entry as "correct" or "incorrect." File revised ITR if income was understated. Submit feedback if AIS entry is erroneous (e.g., duplicate TDS credit).

### 3. Refund Not Received
- **Reason:** Bank account not validated / Aadhaar-PAN link pending / ITR not e-verified.
- **What to do:** Check refund status at incometax.gov.in → Services → Refund Status. Ensure bank account is pre-validated on the portal. Ensure Aadhaar-PAN linking is complete (mandatory since July 2023). Contact CPC Bengaluru at 1800-103-0025.

### 4. Non-Credit of TDS
- **Reason:** "Employer / bank has not deposited TDS with the government / PAN quoted incorrectly."
- **What to do:** Raise a grievance with the employer or bank. If PAN was quoted wrongly, the deductor must revise the TDS return. File ITR based on actual income; attach a note that TDS credit is pending.

### 5. Late Fee Under Section 234F
- **Reason:** Return filed after 31 July of the Assessment Year.
- **Fee:** ₹1,000 (income ≤ ₹5 lakh) or ₹5,000 (income > ₹5 lakh). This is mandatory and cannot be waived except in cases of natural calamity or genuine hardship (application to CBDT required).

### 6. Scrutiny / Assessment Notice
- **Reason:** ITR selected for scrutiny by CBDT risk management system (e.g., high deductions vs. income, mismatch with third-party data).
- **What to do:** Respond to notices within the stated deadline. Provide documentary evidence of claimed deductions (investment proofs, rent receipts, loan statements). You can appear in person or appoint a Chartered Accountant as authorized representative.

## 10. PAN-Aadhaar Linking

Since July 1, 2023, **PAN becomes inoperative** if not linked with Aadhaar. Consequences:
- TDS deducted at higher rate (20%)
- Cannot file ITR
- Pending refunds will not be released

To link: Visit incometax.gov.in → Link Aadhaar. Fee: ₹1,000 (as of FY 2023-24).
