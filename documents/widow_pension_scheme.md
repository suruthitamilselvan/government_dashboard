# Widow Pension Scheme — Complete Guide

**Scheme Name:** Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (IGNWPS) / State Widow Pension  
**Nodal Ministry:** Ministry of Rural Development (NSAP); State Social Welfare Departments  
**Governed By:** National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP), 1995; State Widow Welfare Rules  
**Source:** Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India

---

## 1. Overview

The Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (IGNWPS) is a centrally sponsored scheme under the National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP) that provides monthly financial assistance to widowed women from Below Poverty Line (BPL) households. The scheme recognises the acute economic vulnerability faced by widows, particularly in rural areas, who often lose their primary source of income and social support upon the death of their spouse.

Many states supplement the central pension with their own contribution, significantly increasing the monthly benefit. Additionally, states have standalone widow welfare schemes with varying eligibility norms.

---

## 2. Eligibility Criteria

### Central Component (IGNWPS)
| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Marital Status** | Widowed (spouse must be deceased; divorce / separation does NOT qualify under IGNWPS) |
| **Age** | 40 years to 79 years (central scheme); most states extend down to 18 years |
| **BPL Status** | Household must be in the BPL list / SECC 2011 database |
| **Residence** | Citizen of India; permanent resident of the applying state |
| **Other pension** | Must NOT be receiving any other central government pension simultaneously |
| **Remarriage** | Pension ceases upon remarriage (must be reported to the issuing authority) |

### Age Relaxation Under State Schemes
- Many states (e.g., Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Rajasthan) cover widows from **18 years** onwards under their state widow welfare schemes.
- Upper age limit varies: once a beneficiary turns 80, she typically transitions to the **Old Age Pension** (IGNOAPS) scheme, which offers a higher central share.

---

## 3. Pension Amount

| Age Group | Central Government Share | Typical Total (Central + State) |
|---|---|---|
| 40–79 years | ₹300/month | ₹500–₹2,500/month (state-dependent) |
| On turning 80 | Transitions to IGNOAPS | ₹500 central + state top-up |

> **Note:** States like Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Odisha significantly top up the pension. Check your state's Social Welfare Department portal for the exact amount applicable.

## 4. Documents Required

1. **Death Certificate of Husband:** Mandatory — issued by the Municipal Corporation / Gram Panchayat. This is the single most critical document.
2. **Age Proof of Applicant** (any one):
   - Birth certificate, school leaving certificate, Aadhaar card (with DOB), voter ID (age must be verifiable)
3. **Identity Proof:** Aadhaar card (mandatory for DBT credit)
4. **BPL Status Proof** (any one):
   - BPL ration card (AAY/PHH), or certificate from Gram Panchayat Head / Ward Member confirming BPL status, or SECC 2011 database inclusion
5. **Residence Proof:** Aadhaar, voter ID, or ration card with current address
6. **Bank Account Details:** Bank passbook copy; account must be in the applicant's own name (not a joint account with the deceased); must be Aadhaar-linked
7. **Passport-size photograph** (2–4 copies, recent)
8. **Self-declaration of widowhood** and non-receipt of any other pension — on ₹50/₹100 stamp paper or plain paper (state-specific)
9. **Marriage proof** (if required by state): Marriage certificate or declaration from two witnesses
10. **Income certificate** (in some states): Confirming household income is below the BPL threshold

## 5. Application Process

### Step 1 — Collect Application Form
- **Rural:** From Gram Panchayat office or Anganwadi centre
- **Urban:** From Ward Office / Municipal Corporation / ULB social welfare desk
- **Online:** State Social Welfare portal (e.g., apsocialwelfare.ap.gov.in for Andhra Pradesh, sspmis.bihar.gov.in for Bihar)

### Step 2 — Fill the Form Carefully
- Use capital letters throughout.
- Ensure the applicant's name matches exactly across all documents.
- Provide a working mobile number for OTP and verification.

### Step 3 — Submit with Documents
- Submit the completed form at Gram Panchayat (rural) or Ward Office (urban).
- Obtain a dated acknowledgement receipt. Note the application/reference number.

### Step 4 — Field Verification
- A Gram Panchayat / Urban ward officer conducts an in-person visit to verify:
  - Applicant's identity
  - Her widowhood status
  - Residence at the stated address
  - Approximate economic condition (BPL assessment)

### Step 5 — Approval and Disbursement
- Application forwarded to Block Development Officer (rural) or Sub-Divisional Magistrate (urban) for final sanction.
- On approval, pension is credited directly to the Aadhaar-linked bank account (DBT).
- Disbursement is typically monthly or quarterly depending on the state.
- Approved beneficiaries are registered in the NSAP MIS.

## 6. Common Rejection Reasons and Remedies

The following are the most frequently cited reasons for rejection of widow pension applications, along with clear steps to rectify each:

### 1. Death Certificate of Husband Not Submitted
- **Rejection reason:** "Application rejected — death certificate of spouse not provided."
- **Remedy:** A death certificate is **mandatory and non-negotiable.** Obtain it from the Municipal Corporation or Gram Panchayat where the death was registered. If the death was not registered, approach the local registrar with two witnesses and hospital/cremation documents. A late registration is possible with a magistrate's order (for deaths more than 1 year old).

### 2. Applicant Not Widowed — Divorced or Separated
- **Rejection reason:** "IGNWPS is not applicable to divorced / separated women. Only widows qualify."
- **Remedy:** Apply under **state-specific deserted women / destitute women schemes**, which cover divorced, separated, and abandoned women. These are separate from IGNWPS. Check your state's Social Welfare Department for the relevant scheme name and form.

### 3. Age Does Not Meet Minimum Threshold
- **Rejection reason:** "Applicant's age is below the minimum prescribed age of 40 years (central scheme) / 18 years (state scheme). Age: [X] years."
- **Remedy:** Apply under the applicable state scheme if you are between 18–39 years. If the central scheme age bar is the issue and the applicant is close to 40, the Gram Panchayat may ask her to reapply closer to the eligibility date.

### 4. Bank Account Not in Applicant's Name
- **Rejection reason:** "Bank account is a joint account with the deceased / in another person's name — DBT cannot be processed."
- **Remedy:** Open a new individual bank account in the applicant's own name at the nearest bank or post office. Link it with Aadhaar. Some banks under the Jan Dhan Yojana offer zero-balance accounts specifically for this purpose.

### 5. Not on BPL List / SECC Database
- **Rejection reason:** "Applicant's household is not listed in the BPL / SECC 2011 database."
- **Remedy:** Verify your SECC status at secc.gov.in. If erroneously excluded, submit a representation to the Gram Sabha or District Collector for inclusion in the next BPL survey. Some states accept a poverty certificate from the Gram Panchayat head as an interim measure while the correction is processed.

### 6. Aadhaar Not Linked to Bank Account
- **Rejection reason:** "DBT credit failed — Aadhaar not seeded to the bank account."
- **Remedy:** Visit the bank with Aadhaar card and passbook to seed Aadhaar. Verify on the UIDAI portal. Typically resolved within 5–7 banking days.

### 7. Name Mismatch Across Documents
- **Rejection reason:** "Name on death certificate does not match name in ration card / Aadhaar — relationship cannot be verified."
- **Remedy:** Submit an affidavit on stamp paper (attested by a notary) stating that both names refer to the same person and clarifying the spelling variation. Also attach any document linking the applicant to the deceased (e.g., joint ration card entry, bank nomination documents).

### 8. Receiving Another Government Pension
- **Rejection reason:** "Applicant is already a beneficiary of [IGNOAPS/State Pension Scheme] — dual pension not permitted."
- **Remedy:** Review whether you can surrender the other pension if the widow pension offers a better amount. In some states, IGNWPS and a state widow scheme are mutually exclusive; in others, a state widow scheme can be received alongside a small EPFO pension. Check state rules carefully.

### 9. Field Verification Failure (Not Found at Address)
- **Rejection reason:** "Verification officer visited the stated address — applicant not present."
- **Remedy:** Provide a mobile number on the form and ensure someone is at home during business hours. If the officer was not able to contact the applicant despite visiting, submit a written explanation and request a re-verification date from the Gram Panchayat.

### 10. Incomplete Photograph or Document Quality
- **Rejection reason:** "Photograph is not recent / blurred / does not meet requirements."
- **Remedy:** Submit a recent passport-size photograph with white/light background, taken within the last 6 months. Do not submit photocopies of photographs.

## 7. Special Provisions for Vulnerable Widows

### Destitute and Abandoned Women
Women who are separated, abandoned, or divorced (not widowed) can apply under:
- **State Destitute Women Pension Schemes** (available in most states)
- **Mahila Shakti Kendra (MSK)** for counselling and support
- **One Stop Centres (OSC)** under Ministry of WCD for women in distress

### Widows of Defence Personnel
- Covered under separate schemes by the **Ministry of Defence / Kendriya Sainik Board**
- Liberal pension, housing, and education benefits apply

### Widows of Unorganised Sector Workers
- Eligible for benefits under the PM-SYM (Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan) scheme if registered before death of spouse

---

## 8. Continuation and Renewal

- Pension continues as long as the beneficiary is alive, widowed, and eligible.
- **Annual Life Certificate (Jeevan Pramaan Patra):** Must be submitted every November. Can be submitted at the bank, post office, Common Service Centre, or online via the Jeevan Pramaan app.
- **Obligation to report remarriage:** If the beneficiary remarries, she must inform the issuing authority. Continued receipt of pension after remarriage constitutes fraud and is recoverable.
